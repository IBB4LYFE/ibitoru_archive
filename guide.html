QUICK CONTROLS / 01

Find your way
around.

Join the conversation at your pace. Your account needs admin approval before the shared world opens.

01

Move around

Use WASD or the arrow keys. Click or tap a spot on the floor to walk there. Your avatar and position update for other people in the room.

02

Pick a room

Choose The Lounge, Debate Hall, or Voice Room from the left rail. Select + to name a discussion space; it appears for everyone and is saved for your next visit.

03

Chat together

Type in the room conversation and press Enter to send. Press Shift + Enter for a new line. Messages are shared with people in that room.

04

Use your voice

Join voice and allow microphone access for live conversation. The pink dot records a voice note and sends it automatically after six seconds. Voice is room-based and needs a supported browser.

05

Make your avatar yours

Open the avatar settings icon to choose a color and a look: Star Sprinter, Garden Buddy, or Sound Surfer. Your changes appear to other people in real time.

06

Event and moderation

The official event starts inactive and only an admin can start or end it, approve or remove participants, and assign moderators. Moderators can mute participants and pause or restore their speaking permission.
